The Story

Jin Jun Mei for every day

Qinglu — "Clear Dew" — is our answer to a question we hear constantly from buyers: "I love Jin Jun Mei, but do you have one I can drink every day without checking my bank balance?"

Sourced from high-altitude tea gardens on the outer slopes of Wuyi Mountain, Qinglu is made by the same master tea maker who produces our flagship Wild Mountain Golden Needle. It follows the same core craft — withering, rolling, controlled oxidation, and a gentle roast — but it trades the exclusivity of single-bud plucking for a more forgiving one-bud-one-leaf standard. The result? A Jin Jun Mei that retains the soul of Wuyi black tea while being genuinely affordable — and genuinely hard to brew badly.

Tasting Notes

Clean, sweet, effortless

🍂

Dry Leaf

Tightly rolled, slender leaves with a healthy proportion of golden tips. Dark brown to jet black, clean and uniform. A fine everyday Jin Jun Mei should look the part — and Qinglu does.

👃

Aroma

Warm, inviting, and instantly familiar. Baked sweet potato, caramelized sugar, and a subtle dried fruit sweetness. Once brewed, it deepens into honeyed malt with a faint floral lift — no smoke, no earth, just pure Wuyi comfort.

✨

Liquor

Bright amber-gold, brilliantly clear from the first steep to the last. No cloudiness, no sediment. It pours like liquid sunshine and looks beautiful in both a gaiwan and a simple glass mug.

👅

Flavor

  • 1st steep: Gentle, rounded sweetness — caramel and honey. Nothing sharp, nothing bitter.
  • 2nd–4th: Hints of dried jujube and malt. Clean and bright, with a soft, quenching finish.
  • 5th–6th: Gradually softens, mildly sweet aftertaste. The mark of honest, well-made tea.
